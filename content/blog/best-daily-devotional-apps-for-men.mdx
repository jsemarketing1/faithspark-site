## Why Men Quit Devotionals

Men aren't less spiritual than women. We just come at it differently. Most of us want to know three things: what is this passage saying, what does it ask of me, and how do I do that today? We're not looking for a feeling. We're looking for a direction.

When a devotional hands us warm language and a vague takeaway like "rest in God's love today," we nod, put the phone down, and go on with our day unchanged. Not because we don't care. Because there was nothing to hold onto.

A good men's daily devotional app closes that gap. It reads the passage, tells you what it means, and then gives you something specific: what to do differently, what to pray about, what to say to your wife tonight, what to fight today.

## A Short Daily Devotional for Men

Here's the kind of thing you'd read in the app. Takes about two minutes.

**Read: Joshua 1:9.** "Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the Lord your God will be with you wherever you go."

God said this to Joshua right after Moses died. Joshua was about to lead a whole nation into a land full of fights he didn't ask for. Notice God didn't say "you've got this." He said "I've got you." The courage wasn't supposed to come from Joshua. It was supposed to come from who was going with him.

Most mornings I don't feel strong. I feel tired, behind, and short on patience before I've even pulled out of the yard. But strong and courageous isn't a feeling God is asking me to have. It's a direction He's telling me to walk, because He's already out there ahead of me.

**Do this today:** Name the one thing you've been avoiding because you're afraid of how it'll go. A hard talk with your wife. A call you owe your kid. A temptation you keep negotiating with. Take one step on it before tonight.

**Pray:** Lord, I don't feel strong today, but You're with me wherever I go. Give me the courage to take one step on the thing I've been avoiding. Amen.

That's it. Short, honest, and something to actually do. That's what a men's daily devotional should be.
